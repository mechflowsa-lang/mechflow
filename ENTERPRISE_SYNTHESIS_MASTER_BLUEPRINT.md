### 6.3 Master Services Hub & 6 Dedicated Division Pages (`/services/`)

#### Master Services Index (`/services/index.html`)
Comprehensive overview matrix providing direct pathways into all 6 specialized engineering division pages.

#### Division 1: Turbomachinery & Rotating Assets (`/services/turbomachinery-overhaul.html`)
* **API 610 Centrifugal & Multistage Process Pumps:** Complete teardown, impeller re-balancing, shaft runout straightening, hydrostatic casing pressure tests.
* **API 611/612 Steam Turbines & Centrifugal Compressors:** Diaphragm overhaul, rotor blading refurbishment, journal/thrust bearing replacement.
* **Wireless Laser Shaft Alignment:** Dual-laser Prüftechnik/Easy-Laser alignment eliminating thermal growth offsets.
* **Dynamic Balancing Stand:** In-shop balancing on Schenck balancing machines up to 10 tons (ISO G0.4 - G2.5) + onsite 2-plane field dynamic balancing.

#### Division 2: Fluid & Gas Sealing Technologies (`/services/fluid-gas-sealing.html`)
* **API 682 Heavy-Duty Mechanical Seals:** Face re-lapping (SiC, Tungsten Carbide, Carbon), secondary elastomer upgrades (Kalrez/FFKM).
* **API 692 Dry Gas Seals (DGS):** Cleanroom ISO Class 7 assembly, static leak tests, and dynamic Nitrogen test runs up to 150 bar.
* **Optical Flat Helium Monochromatic Inspection:** Measurement of seal face flatness within 0.3 - 0.6 microns (1-2 helium light bands).
* **Root Cause Failure Analysis (RCFA):** Microscopic thermal and cavitation diagnostic reports with MTBF improvement recommendations.

#### Division 3: Plant Turnaround & Specialized Field Services (`/services/plant-turnaround-field-services.html`)
* **Plant Turnaround (TAR) Execution:** Critical-path scheduling, blinding/de-blinding, internal column tray work, and fast-track valve change-outs.
* **Hydraulic Bolt Torquing & Tensioning:** Simultaneous multi-stud tensioning according to ASME PCC-1 standards.
* **Onsite Flange Facing & Pipe Cold Cutting:** Portable flange facers machining 2" to 120" flanges with standard gramophone finish.
* **High-Pressure Hydrostatic Testing:** Hydro-testing up to 40,000 PSI with calibrated circular chart recorders.
* **Chemical Cleaning & Stainless Steel Pickling:** Inhibited acid circulation and passivation for process heat exchangers and pipe loops.

#### Division 4: Advanced Engineering, Simulation & RCFA (`/services/advanced-engineering-simulation.html`)
* **Finite Element Analysis (FEA):** Structural stress, thermal gradient, and cyclic fatigue simulations.
* **Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD):** Impeller fluid dynamics, recirculation mitigation, and NPSH cavitation optimization.
* **3D Laser Scanning & Reverse Engineering:** High-accuracy FaroArm / 3D optical scanning to reverse engineer legacy or discontinued OEM parts.
* **Automated HVAF Thermal Spray Coatings:** Tungsten carbide wear-resistant coatings for harsh Saudi fluid environments.

#### Division 5: Electrical, Motor & Automation Services (`/services/electrical-motor-automation.html`)
* **Electric Motor Rewinding:** Low and medium voltage AC/DC motor rewinding, Class H insulation, and Vacuum Pressure Impregnation (VPI).
* **VFD / VSD Drive Systems:** Drive setup, harmonic filtration, and pump protection loop tuning.
* **PLC / SCADA Control Automation:** Custom pump control panel assembly, soft starters, and telemetry integration.

#### Division 6: Heat Transfer & Industrial Equipment (`/services/heat-transfer-services.html`)
* **Shell & Tube Heat Exchangers:** Bundle extraction, automated retubing, tube expansion, and hydro-testing.
* **Plate Heat Exchangers:** Ultrasonic chemical wash, gasket replacement, and hydraulic re-clamping.
* **Industrial Cooling Towers & Air Coolers:** Fan gearboxes, drive shafts, and dynamic vibration balancing.
