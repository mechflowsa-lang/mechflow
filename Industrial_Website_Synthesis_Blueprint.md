## 5. 🔬 Deep Service Pillar & Division Specifications

### Division 1: Rotating Equipment & Turbomachinery
* **Centrifugal & Multistage Pump Overhaul:** Full teardown, non-destructive testing (NDT), impeller wear ring replacement, shaft straightening, dynamic balancing, and hydrostatic pressure testing according to API 610 standards.
* **Steam Turbines & Gas Compressors:** Rotor refurbishment, blading inspection, diaphragm repair, bearing replacement, labyrinth seal upgrades, and API 612 / API 617 alignment protocols.
* **Laser Shaft Alignment:** Utilizing dual-laser wireless alignment systems (Prüftechnik / Easy-Laser) to eliminate parallel and angular misalignment, extending bearing and mechanical seal lifespan by up to 400%.
* **Dynamic Balancing:** In-shop balancing on universal hard-bearing machines up to 10,000 kg, plus 2-plane field dynamic balancing directly on operating plant equipment without full disassembly.

### Division 2: Fluid & Gas Sealing Technologies
* **Mechanical Seal Refurbishment:** Re-lapping of Silicon Carbide (SiC), Tungsten Carbide (TC), and Carbon faces to 2 light bands flatness. Replacement of secondary elastomers (FFKM/Kalrez, Viton, Aflas) and spring packs.
* **Dry Gas Seals (DGS) for Compressors:** Dedicated positive-pressure cleanroom facility for assembly, static leak test, and dynamic functional testing of single, tandem, and tandem with intermediate labyrinth DGS configurations according to API 692.
* **Root Cause Failure Analysis (RCFA):** Microscopic and thermal analysis of failed seal faces (thermal cracking, blistering, erosive grooving, vaporization) to deliver permanent design modifications and extended MTBF.

### Division 3: Plant Turnarounds & Specialized Field Services
* **Shutdown & Turnaround (TAR) Management:** End-to-end mechanical execution, blinding/de-blinding, internal column tray inspection, valve change-outs, and rapid-turnaround rotating asset overhauls under strict critical-path schedules.
* **Hydraulic Bolt Torquing & Tensioning:** Controlled hydraulic torquing tools and subsea/topside bolt tensioners ensuring leak-free flange joints per ASME PCC-1 guidelines.
* **Onsite Field Machining:** Portable flange facers, pipe cold bevelers, line boring machines, and milling heads enabling in-situ repairs without removing heavy equipment from plant structures.
* **Chemical Cleaning & Passivation:** Specialized circulation of inhibited acid solutions, neutralizers, and passivation chemicals to restore internal heat exchanger and pipeline cleanliness.

### Division 4: Electrical, Motor & Automation Services
* **Electric Motor Rewinding:** Class H / Class F insulation rewinding for low and medium voltage AC/DC motors, vacuum pressure impregnation (VPI), surge comparison testing, and core loss analysis.
* **VFD & Drive Systems:** Variable frequency drive setup, harmonics mitigation, PID loop tuning, and pump protection integration.
* **Automation & Instrumentation:** Calibration of pressure/temperature transmitters, vibration probes (Bentley Nevada), valve positioners, and PLC/SCADA interface programming.

### Division 5: Advanced Engineering & Reverse Engineering
* **FEA & CFD Simulation:** Finite Element Analysis for thermal stress, cyclic fatigue, and modal vibration. CFD modeling for pump impeller flow optimization and cavitation reduction.
* **3D Laser Scanning & CMM Inspection:** High-accuracy FaroArm / 3D optical scanning to generate precise CAD models for discontinued or long-lead-time OEM components.
* **HVAF Thermal Spraying:** High-Velocity Air-Fuel tungsten carbide coatings for shafts, sleeves, and wear rings operating in highly erosive or corrosive Saudi oil & water environments.
