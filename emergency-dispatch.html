How Our Emergency Response Works

From initial telemetry and triage to on-site restoration, our ISO-certified emergency workflow minimizes unplanned downtime.

STEP 01 • 0 - 5 MINUTES

Immediate Triage & Telemetry

Direct intake with a Senior Rotating Equipment Engineer. Review of asset tag, symptom telemetry, vibration spectrum, or seal face leakage data to define the exact field tooling package.

STEP 02 • 5 - 60 MINUTES

Squad & Tooling Mobilization

Emergency mobile squad dispatches from the nearest hub (Jeddah, Dammam, or Yanbu) equipped with wireless laser alignment kits, portable balancing rigs, and replacement seal cartridges.

STEP 03 • 2 - 4 HOURS

On-Site Overhaul & Commissioning

In-situ mechanical overhaul, dynamic balancing to ISO G2.5, ASME PCC-1 bolt tensioning, and dynamic seal commissioning with full vibration baseline verification before sign-off.
