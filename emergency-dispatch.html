How Our Emergency Response Works

From initial telemetry and triage to on-site restoration, our ISO-certified emergency workflow minimizes unplanned downtime.

STEP 01 • 0 - 5 MINUTES

Immediate Triage & Telemetry

Direct intake with a Senior Rotating Equipment Engineer. Review of asset tag, symptom telemetry, vibration spectrum, or seal face leakage data to define the exact field tooling package.

STEP 02 • 5 - 60 MINUTES
