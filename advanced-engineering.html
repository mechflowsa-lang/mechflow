Advanced Metrology, Simulation & Coatings

Eliminating chronic equipment failures through digital twin physics simulation, precision reverse engineering, and advanced metallurgy across Saudi industrial plants.

7-Axis FaroArm 3D Laser Metrology & Reverse Engineering

Contactless laser line scanning with ±0.025 mm accuracy to digitize worn or obsolete impellers, diffuser rings, and turbine blading for 5-axis CNC manufacturing in Super Duplex and Inconel alloys.

Finite Element Analysis (FEA) Stress & Fatigue Modeling

Simulating high-cycle mechanical fatigue, thermal transient gradients, and modal resonance frequencies to eliminate chronic shaft fractures and pump casing cracking.

Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) & Cavitation Mitigation

Multiphase fluid flow modeling to optimize suction NPSH performance, eliminate recirculation vortices, and redesign vane profiles for maximum hydraulic efficiency.

Supersonic HVAF & HVOF Thermal Spray Coatings

Tungsten carbide (WC-Co-Cr) and Stellite supersonic cladding delivering extreme wear, erosion, and chemical corrosion resistance for high-velocity slurry and seawater assets.
