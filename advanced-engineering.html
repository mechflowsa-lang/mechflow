Digital Twin & Simulation

Precision CAE & Metrology Solutions

MechFlow’s central reverse engineering laboratory in Jeddah supports plants across Saudi Arabia by digitizing complex components, eliminating long OEM procurement lead times, and optimizing hydraulic performance through digital simulation.

• Finite Element Analysis (FEA) Structural & Thermal Stress

Simulating non-linear thermal expansion, centrifugal load stresses, and cyclic fatigue life on pump shafts, impellers, and compressor rotors.

• Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) Cavitation & Flow

Advanced fluid dynamics modeling to eliminate flow recirculation, reduce NPSH required, and extend hydraulic operating efficiency.

• 3D Laser Scanning & CMM Reverse Engineering

Sub-millimeter point cloud scanning with FaroArm CMM arms to generate full CAD models for discontinued, obsolete, or high-cost OEM parts.

• Automated HVAF Tungsten Carbide Protective Spray

High-Velocity Air-Fuel robotic coating delivering ultra-dense, low-porosity tungsten carbide wear layers for corrosive seawater and crude oil service.

  • Thermal Spray Coating: HVAF Tungsten Carbide
  • CAE Software: ANSYS FEA / CFD
