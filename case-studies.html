Power & Utilities • Western Province

14-Stage High-Pressure Boiler Feed Pump Re-Engineering (+350% MTBF)

Operational Challenge: A Rabigh coastal power station experienced chronic pump trips every 5 months due to severe hydraulic cavitation, impeller erosion, and shaft deflection under high differential pressure.
MechFlow Engineering Solution: Reverse-engineered impellers in Super Duplex (UNS S32750), dynamic balancing to ISO G1.0 on our 10-ton Schenck rig, upgraded to API 682 Plan 53B dual pressurized cartridge seals, and precision laser shaft alignment.
Verified Impact: Continuous operation extended from 5 to 24+ months without maintenance intervention, preventing over $680,000 in unplanned outage losses.

Chemical & Petrochemicals • Jubail Industrial City

High-Pressure Hydrogen Compressor Dry Gas Seal Retrofit (API 692)

Operational Challenge: A Jubail synthesis gas plant suffered chronic hydrogen leakage across legacy wet seals at 110-bar operating pressure, causing process gas flaring and high seal oil contamination costs.
MechFlow Engineering Solution: Engineered a turnkey conversion from wet seals to API 692 Tandem Dry Gas Seals with intermediate nitrogen buffer labyrinth. Precision helium optical flat lapping (2 light bands) and 150-bar dynamic nitrogen run verification in our ISO Class 7 cleanroom.
Verified Impact: Zero flaring achieved, $450,000 annual seal oil savings, and seamless continuous operation across a 36-month run cycle.

6.6kV 3,500kW SAG Mill Motor Emergency Rewind & VPI in 9 Days
