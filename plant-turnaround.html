Turnaround Management & Mobile Machining

MechFlow provides specialized turnaround management teams equipped with certified mobile tooling containers, hydraulic torque wrenches, cold cutting saws, and hydro-test pumps deployed directly to your plant site in Jeddah, Yanbu, Jubail, or Dammam.

• Turnaround (TAR) & Shutdown Mechanical Execution

Critical-path scheduling, blinding/de-blinding operations, column internal tray inspections, valve replacement, and rotating asset overhauls under strict 24/7 shifts.

• Controlled Hydraulic Bolt Torquing & Tensioning

Calibrated low-profile and square-drive hydraulic torque wrenches and multi-stud bolt tensioners up to 4" diameter complying with ASME PCC-1 leak-free joint assembly guidelines.

• Onsite In-Situ Flange Facing & Pipe Cold Cutting

Portable ID and OD mounted flange facers capable of machining raised face, flat face, and RTJ ring joint flanges from 2" to 120" diameter with precise spiral gramophone finishes.

Circulation of inhibited acids, neutralizers, and passivation chemicals to eliminate scale, rust, and weld discoloration on stainless steel systems.
