Root Cause Knowledge Base

Failure Signatures & Solutions

Quick engineering reference for critical rotating machinery anomalies.

API 610 Pumps

1X & 2X Unbalance / Misalignment

Symptom: Elevated 1X RPM radial vibration & bearing race pitting.
Protocol: Dual-laser shaft alignment & in-situ dynamic balancing to ISO G2.5.

Laser Alignment + Dynamic Balancing
API 682/692 Seals

Seal Face Thermal Distortion

Symptom: Barrier fluid loss, face grooving & gas leakage.
Protocol: Cleanroom diamond re-lapping & 150-bar dynamic nitrogen run.

Cleanroom Lapping + N2 Test
API 612 Turbines

Thrust Surge & Blade Rub

Symptom: Axial rotor displacement & babbit bearing wipe.
Protocol: Laser geometric survey, rotor NDT & casing remachining.

Geometric Survey + Rotor NDT
LV / MV Motors

Winding Insulation Breakdown

Symptom: Thermal degradation & phase-to-ground turn shorts.
Protocol: Class H (180°C) rewinding & 13.8kV dual-VPI treatment.

Class H Rewind + 13.8kV VPI
Flanged Joints

Thermal Relaxation & Gasket Creep

Symptom: Flange rotation, gasket crush & runaway VOC leaks.
Protocol: Mobile flange facing & ASME PCC-1 hydraulic stud tensioning.

Flange Facing + Hydraulic Bolting
Compressors

Oil Whirl & Aerodynamic Surge

Symptom: Severe 0.43X subsynchronous vibration & machine trip.
Protocol: Modal FEA/CFD simulation, HVAF coating & bearing tuning.

Modal FEA/CFD + HVAF Coating
