Root Cause Knowledge Base

Common Rotating Asset Failure Modes

Engineering reference guide for key failure signatures encountered in petrochemical, power, and desalination plants.

API 610 Centrifugal Pumps

1X & 2X Shaft Misalignment / Unbalance

Causes elevated 1X RPM radial vibration, bearing race pitting, and premature seal face grooving. Requires wireless dual-laser shaft alignment and in-situ balancing to ISO G2.5 standard.

Recommended: Laser Alignment + Dynamic Balancing
API 682/692 Sealing Systems

Dry Gas Seal Face Thermal Distortion

Occurs during low flow or barrier gas contamination leading to groove clogging, frictional heat checking, and dynamic gas blowout. Requires ISO Class 7 cleanroom re-lapping and 150-bar dynamic nitrogen testing.

Recommended: Cleanroom Seal Lapping + Nitrogen Run
API 612 Steam / Gas Turbines

Axial Thrust Bearing Surge & Blade Rub

Steam contamination or balance piston seal wear shifts the rotor axially, causing babbit bearing wipe and diaphragm rubbing. Requires laser geometry survey, rotor NDT, and casing remachining.

Recommended: Rotor NDT + Diaphragm Laser Survey
MV / HV Induction Motors

Stator Winding Insulation Breakdown

Thermal cycling and VFD harmonic spikes degrade magnet wire enamel, resulting in phase-to-ground turn shorts. Requires Class H mica/polyimide rewinding and high-vacuum pressure impregnation (VPI).

Recommended: Class H Rewind + 13.8kV VPI Treatment
Flanged Pipeline Joints

Thermal Relaxation & Gasket Creep (ASME PCC-1)

Uneven manual torquing during turnaround causes flange rotation, gasket crush, and runaway VOC leaks under thermal cycling. Requires mobile flange facing and calibrated multi-stud hydraulic tensioning.

Recommended: Mobile Flange Facing + Hydraulic Bolting
High-Pressure Compressors

Subharmonic Oil Whirl & Aerodynamic Surge

Tilting pad bearing instability and impeller shroud erosion cause severe 0.43X subsynchronous vibration and machine trip. Requires full rotor modal analysis, HVAF tungsten carbide coating, and bearing optimization.

Recommended: Modal FEA / CFD + HVAF Coating
