Full Lifecycle Capabilities

Integrated Engineering & Plant Solutions

Complete lifecycle support for mission-critical industrial assets, from mechanical seals and turbomachinery to turnarounds and digital twins.

Division 01

Turbomachinery & Pumps

Overhaul, re-engineering, and dynamic balancing of API 610 process pumps, multistage boiler feed pumps, steam turbines, and gearboxes.

10-Ton Schenck dynamic balancing up to ISO G2.5
Wireless continuous multi-plane laser alignment
Explore Division Capabilities
Division 02

Fluid & Gas Sealing

Cartridge mechanical seals and Dry Gas Seals (API 692) for centrifugal gas compressors with cleanroom assembly and monochromatic helium lapping.

150-bar dynamic nitrogen gas seal test rig
Optical flatness lapping < 2 helium light bands
Explore Sealing Technology
Division 03

Plant Turnaround (TAR)

Critical path TAR execution, ASME PCC-1 calibrated torque/tensioning, portable on-site flange facing, and high-pressure testing.

Mobile flange facing from 2" to 120" diameter
Hydrostatic pressure testing up to 40,000 PSI
Explore TAR Services
Division 04

FEA, CFD & 3D Scanning

Stress FEA, cavitation CFD, 7-axis FaroArm laser scanning, and thermal spray HVAF coatings for extreme erosion/corrosion environments.

Metrology scanning accurate to ±0.025 mm
HVAF tungsten carbide erosion coatings
Explore Engineering & Simulation
Division 05

Motor Rewinding & VFD

Rewinding of LV & MV electric motors up to 13.8kV / 5,000 HP, VPI resin impregnation, dynamic surge testing, and VFD panel integration.

Class H (180°C) copper winding insulation
VPI vacuum pressure resin treatment
Explore Electrical Services
Division 06

Heat Exchangers & Coolers

Hydraulic tube bundle pulling, electronic torque retubing, plate heat exchanger (PHE) ultrasonic cleaning, and cooling tower refurbishment.

TEMA / ASME Sec VIII retubing & testing
Ultrasonic PHE chemical descaling
Explore Heat Transfer
