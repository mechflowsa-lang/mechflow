Engineering Specialization

Critical Rotating Equipment Capabilities

MechFlow’s workshops in Jeddah, Dammam, and Yanbu are equipped with heavy overhead cranes, computerized balancing stands, and precision machining lathes capable of handling multi-stage boiler feed pumps, steam turbine rotors, and heavy industrial gearboxes.

• API 610 Centrifugal & Multistage Pump Overhaul

Full disassembly, dye-penetrant / magnetic particle inspection, shaft straightening, wear ring renewal, and hydrostatic casing pressure verification.

• Steam Turbines (API 611/612) & Gas Compressors

Rotor blading overhaul, diaphragm restoration, journal/thrust bearing replacement, labyrinth seal retrofit, and axial clearance optimization.

• In-House (10T) & On-Site Field Dynamic Balancing

Hard-bearing Schenck balancing machines handling rotors up to 10 tons (ISO 1940 G0.4 - G2.5) + 2-plane field vibration correction on operating plant machinery.

• Wireless Precision Laser Shaft Alignment

Utilizing Prüftechnik and Easy-Laser dual-sensor alignment systems to eliminate thermal growth offset, reducing bearing stress and seal wear by up to 400%.
