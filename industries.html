Industry Solutions

Sectors We Power & Protect

Engineered reliability for mission-critical industrial plants in Saudi Arabia.

Oil, Gas & Refining

High-pressure solutions for H2S sour gas, extreme pressures, and harsh Gulf ambient conditions.

API 610 BB2 / BB3 / BB5 crude charge & export pumps
API 617 centrifugal compressors & API 612 steam turbines
Tandem Dry Gas Seals (API 692) & Plan 53B cartridge overhauls
Primary Standards: API 610 / 682 / 692

Petrochemicals & Chemicals

Sealing and machining for corrosive chemical loops, polymerization reactors, and crackers.

Top-entry reactor mixer & agitator mechanical seals (Plan 54)
Cleanroom optical flat lapping (2 light bands flatness)
ASME PCC-1 flanged joint management during turnarounds
Primary Standards: ASME PCC-1 / API 682

Desalination & Water (SWCC)

Maintenance for SWRO booster pumps, vertical seawater intakes, and energy recovery devices.

Super Duplex (UNS S32750) impeller fabrication & balancing
Cavitation-resistant HVAF tungsten carbide protective coatings
Submersible seawater pump rebuilds and dielectric testing
Primary Standards: ISO 10816-7 / ISO G1.0

Power Generation (SEC / IPPs)

Thermal and combined-cycle support: boiler feed pumps, turbine NDT, and generator overhauls.

Multistage boiler feed water pump re-engineering (+350% MTBF)
Steam turbine diaphragm survey & babbitt bearing rebabbitting
13.8kV generator rotor & stator Class H rewind & VPI treatment
Primary Standards: API 612 / IEEE 522

Mining & Minerals (Ma'aden)

Severe-duty equipment servicing for high-abrasion phosphate, bauxite, and gold processing.

Heavy-duty slurry pump wet-end rebuilds & shaft sleeving
SAG and ball mill drive motor rewinding (up to 3,500kW in 9 days)
10-ton Schenck dynamic balancing of heavy fan & mill rotors
Primary Standards: ASTM G65 / ISO G2.5

Marine & Port Terminals

Fast-turnaround marine propulsion shafting, cooling bundles, and port transfer pumps.

Multi-coupling wireless laser shaft alignment up to 50m offset
On-site portable flange facing & cold pipe machining (2" to 120")
Plate heat exchanger (PHE) chemical re-gasketing & retubing
Primary Standards: IACS Marine / ASME VIII
