Vital Industrial Sectors We Power & Protect

Delivering high-reliability asset support to mission-critical infrastructure across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Oil, Gas & Refining

Petrochemicals & Polymers

Polymerization reactor mixer seals, hot oil thermal pumps, and ultrasonic NDT testing under corrosive Gulf ambient conditions.
